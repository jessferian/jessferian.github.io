How I work

Five principles behind my work.

Understand before solving

I spend time on the problem first: who it affects, what's really causing it, and what we don't know yet.

Connect people and business

A good outcome works for customers and for the business. I look for the place where the two line up.

Challenge assumptions

I question the brief, the rules and my own first ideas, then test them with customers, data and engineering.

Design for the bigger picture

Every feature sits inside a wider system. I think about how a change affects other teams, platforms and the steps around it.

Make complexity understandable

I look for the simplest model that still covers real cases, so complex rules, data and AI make sense to the people using them.

Designing with AI

AI can help. People stay responsible.

I'm designing AI photo enhancement for dealers, where a photo is also a record of a car's condition. The work is less about the Enhance button and more about what comes after it.

A bit about me

I start with the problem, not the screen.

Since 2022 I've worked at Carsales (CAR Group) on Nexgate, the B2B platform car dealerships use to manage stock, prepare listings, publish to marketplaces and read the market.

I'm curious about why people do what they do. A lot of my work starts before the problem is clear, when the answer depends on customers, Product, engineering and other teams seeing the same thing. I like working through that uncertainty together until the direction is clear.

Lately that's meant automation and AI: where they can take work off dealers, and where a person still needs to stay in charge.

    How I got here

    [Your origin story: where you started, what drew you to design, and the moment you first thought about users. Two short paragraphs.]

    Since April 2022, I've been a Senior Product Designer at Carsales (CAR Group), working on Nexgate, formerly AutoGate. It's the B2B platform dealerships use to manage inventory, prepare and publish listings, and make commercial decisions, across Web and iOS.

    My work covers connected workflows: inventory, market insights, promotions, distribution automation, listing creation and editing, and media. The interesting part is usually underneath the screens: the rules, data and business decisions that shape what people can do.

    A lot of my recent work is in AI and automation: photo enhancement, guided photo capture, version history and recovery, and how buyer behaviour could give dealers useful insight. In each case, I focus on making the capability understandable and giving people the right visibility and control.

    I've always been more interested in why a problem exists than in the first solution. That's pulled me towards customer experience, service design and product strategy, and how design connects customer needs, team processes and business goals across the whole service.

    Before Carsales, I designed at Spacetalk, People's Choice and Make it Happen, and before that worked in graphic design and taught at university.
